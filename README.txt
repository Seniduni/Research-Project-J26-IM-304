MR OPERATING THEATRE  -  Progress Presentation 1 (ward + equipment only)
=========================================================================
Open in Unity 6000.3.x. Scenes (Assets/MR_OperatingTheatre/Scenes):
  MR_OperatingTheatre.unity            walk the theatre, click any equipment for its info card
  MR_OperatingTheatre_AssetGallery.unity  every asset and instrument laid out for presentation
Controls: W A S D walk | hold right mouse: look | left click: select | F: foot sensor | H: help
Rebuild materials/prefabs/scenes: menu MR Operating Theatre > 1. Build Ward.
VR: menus "2. Install XR Packages" then "3. Configure OpenXR".
Share: MR Operating Theatre > Export Shared Ward Package. Assets are identical to the
"3D Onboarding & Orientation Module" project. Blender files: "Blender Source" folder
(open MR_OperatingTheatre_Ward.blend; per-asset files in Equipment\ and Instruments\).
DEV HELPERS (safe to delete): Assets/Editor/DevBridge.cs, _incoming, _dev_log.txt
